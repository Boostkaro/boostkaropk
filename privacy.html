Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

BoostKaro ("we", "us") operates the website boostkaropk.com, which sells social media growth services. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Payment references: the transaction ID (TID) of your JazzCash, Easypaisa or NayaPay payment so we can verify it. We never collect or store bank card numbers.

How we use your information

  • To process, deliver and track your order.

Sharing

We share only what is needed to fulfil your order: our delivery partners receive the public profile or post link you provided. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

We keep order records for as long as needed for support, refunds and accounting. You may ask us to delete your personal information at any time (see below).
