What Affects the Price?

1. Follower Quality

Not all followers are equal. Cheap followers are often bot accounts that Instagram removes quickly. Higher-quality followers come from real-looking accounts with profile pictures, posts, and activity.

2. Drop Rate & Refills

"Drop" means followers disappearing over time (Instagram removes fake accounts). Cheaper services have higher drop rates. Better services offer refill guarantees — if followers drop, they replace them free.

3. Delivery Speed

Some services deliver 1,000 followers in an hour. Others spread it over days to look more natural. Faster isn't always better — gradual delivery looks more organic.

4. Targeting

General worldwide followers are cheapest. Country-targeted followers (e.g., Pakistani followers) cost more because they're harder to source.

Red Flags: How to Spot Scams

🚩 Prices too good to be true — 10,000 followers for Rs 500? Those will vanish in days.

🚩 Asks for your password — Legitimate services NEVER need your Instagram password. They only need your username or profile link.

🚩 No refill guarantee — If they won't replace dropped followers, they'll disappear and you're stuck.

🚩 No contact information — Can you reach them on WhatsApp? Do they have a real website?

🚩 Instant 100K delivery — Gaining 100,000 followers in 1 hour looks fake and can trigger Instagram's spam detection.

How to Choose the Right Service

  1. **Start small** — Test with 1,000 followers before buying 10,000
  2. **Check the refill policy** — Minimum 30 days, ideally 60-90 days
  3. **Read the delivery time** — Gradual is safer than instant
  4. **Verify contact info** — WhatsApp number, website, response time
  5. **Never share your password** — This is non-negotiable

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to buy Instagram followers?

Buying followers doesn't require your password, so your account can't be hacked. However, very low-quality followers may be removed by Instagram. Choose services with refill guarantees.

How long do bought followers last?

It depends on quality. Budget followers may drop 20-30% over time. High-quality followers with refill guarantees are replaced if they drop.

Will Instagram ban my account?

Instagram doesn't typically ban accounts for having purchased followers. The risk is low if you buy from reputable sources and don't buy unrealistic amounts instantly.

What's the minimum I should buy?

Start with 1,000 to test the service quality before committing to larger orders.
