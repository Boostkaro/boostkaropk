What Affects the Price of TikTok Followers?

1. Profile Quality

Not all followers are equal. Profiles with pictures, posts, and real activity cost more to source than basic accounts.

2. Drop Rate & Refills

"Drop" means followers disappearing over time. Lower-priced tiers may lose a small percentage and offer no refill; non-drop and refill-backed tiers cost more.

3. Delivery Speed

Faster delivery costs more. High Quality can deliver up to 50K per day, while standard tiers are slower. For smaller accounts, gradual delivery usually looks more natural.
