What Affects the Price of Facebook Followers?

1. Stability & Refills

Followers that stay — backed by non-drop delivery, guarantees and refills — cost more to source than fast, unguaranteed ones.

2. Delivery Speed

Very fast delivery (up to 100K per day) suits large boosts, while steadier speeds look more natural for a growing page. The right speed depends on your page size — a new page jumping by tens of thousands overnight looks less natural than a steady climb.

3. Order Size Limits

Premium tiers cap orders (100K on High Quality profile / page followers) to keep delivery controlled, which is part of why they cost more per 1,000 than bulk tiers.

What Followers Can — and Cannot — Do for Your Page

Be clear about this before you spend money: followers alone do not guarantee reach, sales or monetization. Facebook shows your posts to only a fraction of your followers, and real reach comes from content people actually engage with — comments, shares and watch time on your posts and videos. Followers give you social proof and a stronger first impression; they do not replace regular posting. The pages that succeed treat a follower boost as a head start, then grow with consistent, useful content for their audience.
