How to Choose an SMM Panel in Pakistan

There are hundreds of SMM panels selling to Pakistan, and their quality varies a lot. Before you pay any panel, check these points:

1. Local PKR payments. A panel built for Pakistan should price in rupees and accept JazzCash, Easypaisa or NayaPay. Paying in dollars through foreign gateways adds conversion cost and makes refunds harder.

2. Written quality tiers and refill terms. Cheap followers that drop in a week are not cheap. Look for clearly labelled tiers, honest drop expectations, and refill guarantees written on the service — BoostKaro's No Drop and High Quality tiers carry guarantees of up to 365 days.

3. No password, ever. A legitimate panel only needs your public username or post link. If a site asks for your social media password, leave.

4. Order tracking. You should get an order code and be able to follow your order's status yourself, instead of chasing support for updates. BoostKaro orders can be tracked any time on the Track Order page.

5. Support that actually answers. Test the WhatsApp or support channel with a question before placing a large order. Panels disappear; support is how you judge whether anyone is really there.

6. Realistic prices. Prices far below every competitor usually mean low-quality accounts that drop fast. Compare the full price list — ours is published openly above — and start with a small test order before buying in bulk.

7. Clear refund terms. Read the terms before ordering: what happens if an order cannot be delivered, and which mistakes (such as a wrong or private link) are not refundable.
